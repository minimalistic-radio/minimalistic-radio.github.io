Everything a radio needs. Nothing else.

Left click on the icon opens the player, right click the menu. Your stations, the song that's playing, volume and Stop.

Pick a station to add it to your radio

Stations

Thousands of stations, with suggestions for your country

Search radio-browser.info by name, genre or city. Logos, codec and bitrate at a glance, and drag your list into any order.

Statistics

See what you listen to

Listening time this month, this year or all time, your top stations and artists, and the hours you listen most. Counted only on your computer, and you can switch it off.

Share

Share a station with one link

Found a station you love? Copy its link from the station menu and send it to a friend. One click adds it to their radio.

Song history

Every song the stations announce. Copy it, or find it on Spotify or YouTube.

Sleep timer and media keys

Fall asleep to the radio. Play and Stop from the keyboard or your headphones.

Updates itself

A green arrow shows when a new version is ready. One click and it is done.

Nine languages

English, Polski, Deutsch, Français, Español, Italiano, Português, Čeština and Українська.

Private

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your stations and statistics stay on your computer.

Small and native

One 260 KB .exe on Windows, no installer. Made the native way on every system.
